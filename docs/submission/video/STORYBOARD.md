# AutoMarts Cinematic 4K Showcase - Storyboard

**Target Duration:** Approximately 83 seconds (3840×2160, 30 FPS)

## Production Overview

This storyboard outlines a professional automotive e-commerce promotional video that showcases the actual AutoMarts website with cinematic quality. The video combines real website interactions with automotive imagery to create a compelling brand presentation.

---

## Scene Breakdown

### Scene 1: Brand Introduction (7 seconds)
**Visual Direction:** Cinematic automotive background with AutoMarts logo reveal
- Dark automotive workshop setting with tools and engine parts visible in background
- Soft golden lighting creating professional atmosphere
- AutoMarts logo animates from darkness with subtle motion blur removal
- Automotive elements (oil filter, spark plug, battery) float in as logo appears
- Text: "AutoMarts" in clean, modern sans-serif font

**Audio:** "Welcome to AutoMarts — your trusted destination for genuine auto parts."

### Scene 2: Modern Shopping Experience (8 seconds)
**Visual Direction:** Real website screenshot with smooth scroll animation
- AutoMarts homepage showing current hero section with vehicle finder
- Smooth mouse scroll down showing navigation menu and featured products

- Subtle animated arrows indicating scroll action
- Premium automotive sound effect as transition occurs

**Visual Direction:** Featured products showcase with automotive imagery
- Real product cards from AutoMarts catalog (Bosch spark plugs, Brembo brake pads, etc.)

- Category labels animated in with automotive technical details
- Smooth transition between products

**Audio:** "Browse our catalog of genuine parts — from filters and lubricants to batteries and brake components — all sourced from authorized distributors."

**Visual Direction:** Category navigation with automotive system visualization

- Quantity update controls with automotive-themed styling

**Audio:** "Add parts to your cart and check out seamlessly — guest checkout, real-time inventory, and secure payments ensure a frictionless purchase every time."

## Visual Quality Control

### Cinematography Standards
- All automotive footage uses real AutoMarts website screenshots
- Motion graphics integrated with actual product images
- Consistent automotive color palette (primary: #C41E3A, secondary: #004E9F, accent: #FFD700)
- Clean typography following automotive brand guidelines
- Smooth 30 FPS throughout with professional editing

### Automotive Authenticity
- All product imagery reflects actual AutoMarts catalog
- Category icons match real automotive components
- Technical details based on actual product specifications
- Professional automotive photography style
- Real website interactions (no fabricated flows)

### Audio Standards
- Professional male narrator with automotive expertise voice
- Natural 115-125 words per minute pace
- Subtle background music (when licensed)
- Clear articulation with minimal compression
- Automotive sound effects where appropriate

## Technical Specifications

### Video Format
- Resolution: 3840×2160 (4K)
- Frame Rate: 30 FPS constant
- Codec: H.264 High Profile Level 4.1
- Audio: AAC LC at 48 kHz
- Container: MP4 with fast-start
- Bitrate: ~6 Mbps video, ~128 kbps audio

### Production Workflow
1. Real website recording and editing
2. Automotive visual asset integration
3. Professional voiceover generation and mixing
4. Motion graphics and transitions
5. Color correction and grading
6. Final rendering and quality verification
